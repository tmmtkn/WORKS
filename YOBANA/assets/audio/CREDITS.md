# Recorded fireworks audio

All recordings below are published under CC0 1.0. Copies of the source files are not required at runtime; the edited PCM WAV files are included locally.

## Bloom and crackling embers

- Work: Fireworks Crackle
- Creator: 16FThumaF
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/16FThumaF/sounds/505264/
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded source: public HQ MP3, https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/505/505264_10791958-hq.mp3
- Recorded on a ZOOM H5 with X-Y stereo microphone, as described by the creator.
- Edits: three 6.3-second excerpts starting at 9.15, 22.93 and 62.73 seconds; gentle low-pass filtering, DC removal, peak normalization, edge fades and resampling to 24 kHz stereo PCM.
- Files: bloom-1.wav, bloom-2.wav, bloom-3.wav.

## Ascent

- Work: Whistle and Explosion Single_Firework
- Creator: Rudmer_Rotteveel
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/336008/
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded source: public HQ MP3, https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/336/336008_4921277-hq.mp3
- Recorded on a Tascam DR-05, as described by the creator.
- Edits: first 1.06 seconds only, excluding the explosion; soft edge fades, original 44.1 kHz stereo PCM. Played at its original speed and pitch, without time stretching.
- File: ascent.wav.

Retrieved 2026-10-24. The v2 saved version plays these as natural fireworks. The current ambient experiment uses only the bloom recordings: the opening blast is excluded, tail fragments are slowed and overlapped with smooth envelopes, filtered, and diffused through generated reverberation. The ascent recording remains available for the saved version but is not played.

## Current sound study in film-fireworks.html

After the visual-v3 checkpoint, this preview returns to the real ascent and bloom recordings, with gradual gain envelopes, gentle high/low-pass filtering and a generated, dark 5.2-second reverberation tail. It does not use granular stretching, pitched oscillators or synthetic crackles. The other preview pages retain their ambient experiments. Original WAV assets are unchanged.
